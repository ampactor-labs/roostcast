Roostcast — will this bat house cook?

Bat house guidance still decides paint colour from one number: the average July high. Under 85 °F, paint it black. That rule was written before boxes in full sun were measured at 55 °C, hot enough to kill a colony in an afternoon. This simulates your box instead — hour by hour, chamber by chamber, through a real maternity season at your coordinates.

    What this is. A transient thermal model of a bat house driven by ERA5 reanalysis weather for your coordinates. Nothing is fitted to the field measurements it is tested against; the constants come from ASHRAE surface-property tables and published bat physiology. Source, method and the validation run: tools/validate.js in the repository.

    What it is not. A substitute for a datalogger. Absolute temperatures carry roughly ±5 °C of uncertainty, mostly from local shading and wind that a 9 km reanalysis grid cannot see. The comparisons are far firmer than the absolute numbers, because a modelling error moves every option in the same direction. Use it to choose between configurations, then verify the one you install.
